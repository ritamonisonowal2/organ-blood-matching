How it works

From your first search to a request you can follow.

Search — no login

Choose the recipient's blood group, donation type and city. Explore matching donors with exact-group and same-city preferences.

Send a request

Share your request details. Contact information becomes available after a donor accepts and agrees to share it.

Fulfil & track

Follow the status with your private code. Administrators record completion and the stock updates accordingly.
