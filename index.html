How it works

From registration to transfusion in three steps.

Search — no login

Choose the blood group (or organ) and city. Compatible donors are ranked instantly, exact group first and nearest city next.

Contact the donors

Leave your name and phone, get the donors' numbers and call them. If nobody is available, post an open request — donors see it on their dashboard.

Fulfil & track

The admin marks the request fulfilled and blood stock updates automatically through a database trigger.
